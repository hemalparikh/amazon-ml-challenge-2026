Normalized Blocking Baseline
============================

Dataset:
- Train Source 1: first 10,000 records
- Candidate sources: full Train Source 2 + Source 3
- Ground truth matched by source1_entity_id

Blocking:
- Normalized business name
- Normalized business address
- Country
- Maximum blocking-key frequency: 100

Results:
- Total true links: 34,752
- Captured true links: 14,806
- Blocking recall: 42.6%
- Average candidates: 25.07
- Median candidates: 1
- Maximum candidates: 324

Conclusion:
This is a valid baseline but insufficient for final submission.
A large number of true matches are lost during candidate generation.
